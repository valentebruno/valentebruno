## Selected client work

- **Bosch Power Tools, through ALTEN.** I worked on the iPhone integration for connected robotic lawn mowers. Owners could start or pause mowing remotely and set a schedule from their phone. [Bosch Smart Gardening on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bosch-smart-gardening/id1445549603)
- **Ooredoo Oman, through TIMWE.** I led the move from Xamarin to native Swift for the app customers used to pay bills and manage their mobile plans.
- **Usercentrics.** I improved accessibility in the iOS SDK and added XCTest coverage to catch regressions before SDK releases.
- **Financial Times, through Parser.** I contributed to the original FT Edit app for iOS. It gave readers a daily selection of eight stories chosen by the editors. [How the FT describes the edition](https://www.ft.com/content/b1625614-8fc3-436b-a82f-c13d357aba18)
- **Editora Melhoramentos, through Punch!** I led development of the 2011 iPad edition of Ziraldo’s *O Menino da Terra*. Ziraldo could sign a reader’s copy on the iPad, and the app kept the autograph with the book. [PublishNews coverage of the launch](https://www.publishnews.com.br/materias/2011/09/08/65034-emo-menino-da-terraem-foi-parar-no-ipad)
