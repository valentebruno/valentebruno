# Bruno Valente · Senior iOS Engineer, Apple Platforms Architect & Transmedia Storyteller

I helped bring Gilberto Gil’s Salvador Carnival to viewers on the web. At Punch!, I worked on an iPad book that Ziraldo could autograph for a child, just as he could at a book fair. Today I work with engineering and editorial teams on live video, interactive publishing, and iOS apps that need to keep working through a major change.

## Transmedia storytelling and streaming

- **LitGirlsBr.** Renata Frade and I developed this project at Punch! to connect Brazilian women writing for young readers with their audience. Readers could meet the authors through filmed interviews and book-fair events, then discover their fiction in *O Livro Delas*, the anthology Renata edited for Rocco. [The project in Renata’s words](https://punchcom.wordpress.com/2016/08/01/editorarocco-lanca-o-livro-delas-na-bienal-do-livro-sp-parte-do-projeto-litgirlsbr-da-punchcom/) · [The book at Rocco](https://rocco.com.br/produto/o-livro-delas/)
- **Gilberto Gil’s Carnival webcast.** With Workvideo, I helped the video production team on Gilberto Gil and IBM’s live internet coverage from Salvador. Years later, I designed a WebRTC streaming system for a media company. [Gil recalls the webcast](https://www.jb.com.br/carnaval-2013/noticias/2013/02/08/carnaval-e-invencao-do-diabo-que-deus-abencoou-diz-gilberto-gil/)
- **Mastercard Destino Brasil.** Through Workvideo and Girassol Filmes, I edited and color-graded complete episodes of this cultural travel series for Canal Brasil. I also contributed occasionally to scripts and story development. [Coverage in Folha de S.Paulo](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/folha/turismo/noticias/ult338u569.shtml)

## Selected client work

- **Bosch Power Tools, through ALTEN.** I worked on the iPhone integration for connected robotic lawn mowers. Owners could start or pause mowing remotely and set a schedule from their phone. [Bosch Smart Gardening](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bosch-smart-gardening/id1445549603)
- **Ooredoo Oman, through TIMWE.** I led the migration from Xamarin to native Swift for the app customers used to pay bills and manage their mobile plans.
- **Usercentrics.** I improved accessibility in the iOS SDK and added XCTest coverage to catch regressions before SDK releases.
